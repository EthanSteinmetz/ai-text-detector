Objective

What This Project Set Out to Do

The goal of this project was to replicate the earnings analysis workflow used by equity research analysts and S&T professionals on earnings day. When a major bank like JPMorgan reports quarterly results, analysts must quickly synthesize a large volume of data — comparing actual results to consensus estimates, assessing segment performance, and distilling forward guidance into actionable takeaways.

Using JPMorgan's publicly available Q3 2024 earnings press release and the 10-Q filed with the SEC, I built a structured earnings model in Excel and Python to organize, analyze, and present the key findings in the format a trading desk would expect.

Tools Used

Microsoft Excel Python (pandas) SEC EDGAR Bloomberg Terminal (Academic) JPMorgan 10-Q Earnings Press Release

Key Finance Concepts

  • Earnings Per Share (EPS) — Actual vs. Consensus Estimate
  • Net Interest Income (NII) and Net Interest Margin (NIM)
  • Return on Equity (ROE) and Return on Tangible Common Equity (ROTCE)
  • CET1 Capital Ratio — Basel III regulatory measure
  • Investment Banking (IB) fee revenue — advisory, ECM, DCM
  • Credit provisions — loan loss reserves and charge-off rates
  • Forward guidance and analyst consensus revision dynamics

Methodology

Process & Approach

01

Source the Data

Downloaded the Q3 2024 earnings press release from JPMorgan's investor relations page and the 10-Q from SEC EDGAR. Compiled pre-earnings consensus estimates from FactSet (academic simulation) for EPS, revenue, NII, and key segment metrics.

02

Build the Earnings Model

Structured an Excel model with three tabs: (1) Beat/Miss Summary — actual vs. estimate for all headline metrics; (2) Segment Breakdown — Consumer & Community Banking, CIB, Commercial Banking, Asset & Wealth Management; (3) YoY/QoQ trend analysis for key KPIs.

03

Analyze Segment Performance

Decomposed total revenue and profit by segment to identify which business lines drove the beat. CIB investment banking fees, NII compression in consumer, and AWM AUM flows were the three focal areas for assessment.

04

Assess Forward Guidance

Extracted management's NII guidance update for full-year 2024 and compared to prior guidance. Evaluated implications for consensus estimates and modeled the likely direction of analyst estimate revisions.

05

Write the Earnings Summary

Produced a one-page earnings summary in the format used by S&T desks: headline beat/miss, three key positives, two key risks, and a forward view — all in under 300 words.

Key Findings

Three Positives, Two Risks

Positive #1

IB fee revenue surged 15% above expectations, driven by a rebound in ECM and advisory activity — signaling a broader capital markets recovery.

Positive #2

NII exceeded estimates despite margin compression fears. Full-year NII guidance raised by $500M, implying continued deposit repricing tailwinds.

Positive #3

CET1 capital ratio of 15.3% remains well above regulatory minimums, providing significant flexibility for buybacks and capital return.

Risk #1

Consumer credit quality is deteriorating at the margin. Net charge-off rate in cards rose to 3.74%, above Q2 levels — worth monitoring into 2025.

Risk #2

Noninterest expense guidance implies limited operating leverage in 2025 as the firm continues heavy investment in technology and headcount.

Forward View

Expect consensus EPS estimates for FY2024 to move ~3–5% higher. Rate-cut trajectory and IB pipeline are the key variables to watch in Q4.

What I Learned

Key Takeaways

Bank-Specific Metrics

NII, NIM, CET1, ROTCE — these aren't in standard accounting courses. I learned what each measures and why they drive bank valuations.

Estimate Dynamics

A beat isn't just about the number — the quality of the beat (NII vs. fee income) and guidance revisions often matter more than the headline surprise.

Speed & Synthesis

Earnings analysis requires quickly extracting signal from noise. I practiced condensing 80+ pages of filings into a structured one-pager.

Segment Decomposition

Understanding which business lines drove results is essential to assessing earnings quality and forecasting future quarters.

Why It Matters for Finance

Relevance to S&T and Equity Research

On earnings day, S&T desks and research analysts need to process results in real time — often within minutes of the press release hitting. The ability to quickly parse a large bank's earnings, identify the key beat drivers, and form a view on the stock's near-term reaction is a core professional skill.

This project mimics exactly that workflow. Understanding JPMorgan's business model — how it earns money across consumer, wholesale, and wealth management — provides a foundation for analyzing any large financial institution.

For roles in equity research (financial institutions), S&T (rates and credit), or investment banking (FIG), this type of analysis is a daily deliverable.
